The Ego Calculator is a self-assessment tool designed to help you reflect on confidence, ambition, humility, and reactions to everyday situations. Your answers are used to identify a personality archetype.

Humble Harmonizer

The Humble Harmonizer represents a personality style associated with cooperation, self-awareness, humility, and consideration for others. People who fit this archetype may place importance on maintaining positive relationships while remaining open to feedback.

Confident Achiever

The Confident Achiever represents a personality style associated with self-confidence, persistence, personal responsibility, and a willingness to pursue goals. This archetype emphasizes confidence while maintaining awareness of other people's perspectives.

Ambitious Leader

The Ambitious Leader represents a personality style associated with strong goals, initiative, determination, and leadership tendencies. People who fit this archetype may be highly motivated to take action and pursue challenging objectives.
